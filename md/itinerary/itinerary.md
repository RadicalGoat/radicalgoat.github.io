# Rome Trip: November 2026: Detailed Itinerary

## Friday 6 November — Arrival & Historic Centre

**Theme: Hotel → Pantheon → Piazza Navona → Campo de' Fiori → Trastevere**

**Approximate walking:** 4–4½ miles.

A relatively easy first day after travelling, concentrating on sights close to the hotel.

### 12:30–13:30 — Arrive at Hotel Nazionale

Check in/leave luggage.

### 13:30 — First Roman lunch/snack

Find something appealing as you walk.

Head towards:

**Piazza della Rotonda → Pantheon**

### 14:00–14:45 — Pantheon

Go inside rather than just viewing it from outside.

Allow 30–45 minutes.

### 14:45–15:30 — Piazza Navona

Walk through the historic centre to Piazza Navona.

Take time to look at the fountains, surrounding buildings and street life.

### 15:30–16:15 — Campo de' Fiori & Riverside

Walk towards:

* Campo de' Fiori
* Piazza Farnese
* Via Giulia
* Ponte Sisto

### 16:15–17:00 — Cross the Tiber

Cross **Ponte Sisto** and enter Trastevere.

### 17:00–18:30 — Trastevere

Wander around:

* Piazza di Santa Maria
* Basilica di Santa Maria in Trastevere
* Via della Lungaretta
* Piazza Trilussa

### 18:30–20:00 — Dinner in Trastevere

Possible restaurants:

* Tonnarello
* Mimi e Coco Trast

### 20:00–21:00 — Walk back to hotel

Cross back over the river and walk through the historic centre.

---

# Saturday 7 November — Vatican City

**Theme: Vatican Museums → Sistine Chapel → St Peter's Basilica → St Peter's Square**

**Approximate walking:** 3–4 miles.

This becomes the dedicated Vatican day.

### 07:30–08:00 — Breakfast at hotel

### 08:00 — Leave hotel

Walk or take a short taxi to Vatican City.

I'd favour a taxi if you have an early Vatican Museums booking.

### 08:45 — Arrive at Vatican Museums

Allow time to find the entrance and get through the initial ticket/security procedures.

### 09:00–12:00 — Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel

**BOOK IN ADVANCE**

The visit should include:

* Vatican Museums
* Major galleries
* Gallery of Maps
* Raphael's Rooms
* Sistine Chapel

Don't try to see everything. The Museums are enormous.

Allow approximately **3 hours**.

### 12:00–12:30 — Walk towards St Peter's

Depending on the route available from the Museums, make your way towards St Peter's Basilica.

### 12:30–14:00 — St Peter's Basilica

Visit the Basilica.

Allow approximately 1–1½ hours.

Possible highlights:

* Michelangelo's Pietà
* Bernini's Baldacchino
* Main nave
* Tombs/monuments

### 14:00–14:30 — St Peter's Square

Spend some time in the square and look at Bernini's colonnade.

### 14:30 — Lunch/snack

Have a casual lunch/snack around the Vatican or Prati.

### 15:00–16:00 — Castel Sant'Angelo

Walk down Via della Conciliazione towards:

**Castel Sant'Angelo**

For this itinerary, I'd concentrate on the exterior and the bridge rather than going inside.

### 16:00 onwards — Walk back towards central Rome

Cross the Tiber and head back towards the hotel.

You could go via:

* Ponte Sant'Angelo
* Piazza Navona
* Pantheon

### 19:00/19:30 — Dinner

Dinner near the hotel.

Possible choices:

* CiPASSO
* Armando al Pantheon

---

# Sunday 8 November — Ancient Rome

**Theme: Colosseum → Forum → Palatine → Imperial Forums → Capitoline → Trevi → Spanish Steps**

**Approximate walking:** 4–5 miles.

This is the main Ancient Rome day and should make the most of the available daylight.

### 08:00 — Breakfast at hotel

### 08:30 — Walk towards Colosseum

### 08:45–09:00 — Arrive at Colosseum

**BOOK IN ADVANCE**

Aim for a **09:00 Colosseum entry**.

### 09:00–10:15 — Colosseum

Allow around 75 minutes.

### 10:15–12:30 — Roman Forum & Palatine Hill

Allow approximately 2–2¼ hours.

Don't rush this section.

### 12:30–13:00 — Walk towards Monti

Head through the Monti area.

### 13:00 — Lunch/snack

Have something casual:

* Pizza al taglio
* Panino
* Supplì
* Similar Roman street food

### 13:30–14:30 — Imperial Forums

Walk towards central Rome via:

* Imperial Forums
* Trajan's Column
* Piazza Venezia
* Vittoriano

### 14:30–15:15 — Capitoline Hill

Walk up Michelangelo's staircase.

Look back over the Roman Forum.

### 15:15–16:00 — Piazza Venezia → Trevi Fountain

Walk towards the Trevi Fountain.

See the fountain and, if desired, enter the €2 inner perimeter.

### 16:00–16:30 — Spanish Steps

Continue to the Spanish Steps.

It will be getting dark around this time.

### 16:30 onwards — Evening

Walk back towards the hotel via Via del Corso.

Optionally stop at Trevi again after dark.

### 19:00/19:30 — Dinner

Final proper dinner in Rome.

I'd choose somewhere around:

* Pantheon
* Piazza Navona
* Historic centre

---

# Monday 9 November — Final Morning Near the Hotel

**Theme: Anything missed → Historic Centre → Hotel → Airport**

**Approximate walking:** 2–3 miles.

Keep this morning deliberately flexible.

There is no need to book a major attraction.

### 08:00 — Breakfast

### 09:00–11:30 — Explore anything missed

Possible route:

**Hotel → Piazza Colonna → Trevi Fountain → Spanish Steps → Via del Corso → Piazza Navona → Pantheon → Hotel**

Or concentrate on smaller sights:

* Piazza di Pietra
* Galleria Sciarra
* Largo di Torre Argentina
* Campo de' Fiori
* Any favourite location from the previous three days

This is also an opportunity to revisit somewhere you particularly enjoyed.

### 11:30 — Return to Hotel

Collect luggage.

### 12:00–13:30 — Final free time

Have a final coffee/snack and allow plenty of time before departure.

### 14:30 — Depart Hotel Nazionale

Taxi + Leonardo Express / planned airport transfer.

### 16:00 — Arrive Fiumicino

### 19:10 — Flight to Belfast
