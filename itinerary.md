### Transfers

In order of preference based on cost and predictability:

* Taxi from airport (~50 Euro)
* Train (~28 Euro) + Taxi (~20 Euro)
* Private car (~140 Euro)

Risks:

* Train + Taxi is more predictable in terms of traffic
* Train + Taxi means finding correct train on the way back


Time available: Roughly 2½ days
Overall Plan: Concentrate on the historic centre, Ancient Rome, and the Vatican, with plenty of walking between them.

Considerations:

* Hotel Nazionale is right in the historic centre, between the Pantheon and Trevi Fountain.
* Daylight - on 6 November, sunset in Rome is about **16:58**, so put the major outdoor Ancient Rome sights earlier in the day.
* Vatican Museums are only open Mon–Sat. Sun is normally closed except the last Sun of month — so 8 Nov is not a Vatican Museums day.

### Saturday 7 November — Ancient Rome

**Theme: Colosseum → Forum → Capitoline Hill → Trevi → Spanish Steps**

Busiest day.

**Approximate walking:** 4–5 miles.

#### 08:00 — Breakfast at hotel

Then walk towards the Colosseum.

#### 08:45–09:00 — Arrive at Colosseum

Colosseum: **book in advance**.

Opens: 08:30 (the Roman Forum/Palatine opens at 09:00). 
Closes: 16:30, with last admission at 15:30.

Colosseum entry requires a reserved time slot, with tickets released 30 days ahead. ([Parco archeologico del Colosseo][3])

Cost: 18 Euro

Aim for a **09:00 Colosseum entry**.

STOPPED HERE

Roman Forum

Palatine Hill
